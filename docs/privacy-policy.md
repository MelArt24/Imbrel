# Privacy Policy for Imbrel

**Effective date: September 6, 2026**

Imbrel ("the App") is a weather forecast application that provides current weather conditions, hourly forecasts, daily forecasts, location-based weather information, and weather search by location.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Imbrel accesses and processes, why it is used, how it is handled, and what third-party services are involved.

## 1. Information We Access and Process

### Location Data

Imbrel may request access to your device location in order to provide weather information for your current location.

Depending on the permission granted by you and the capabilities of your device, this may include:

- approximate location;
- precise location.

Location access is optional. You may deny the location permission and use the App by searching for a location manually.

Location data is used only to provide weather-related functionality.

When location-based weather is requested, geographic coordinates may be transmitted through the App's backend infrastructure to the weather data provider in order to retrieve the requested forecast.

Imbrel does not use location data for advertising, profiling, or tracking users across applications.

### Location Search Data

When you search for a city or another location manually, the entered location name may be processed by geocoding or weather-related services in order to determine the appropriate location and retrieve weather information.

Search information is used only for providing the requested weather functionality.

### Locally Stored Data

Imbrel stores certain information locally on your device to improve functionality and allow limited offline access.

This may include:

- cached weather forecasts;
- location information associated with cached weather;
- the most recently used or saved location;
- application preferences.

This information is stored locally on the device and is not used to create a user profile.

You can remove locally stored Imbrel data by clearing the App's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

### Network and Technical Data

When Imbrel communicates with online services, service providers involved in delivering those requests may automatically process technical information such as:

- IP address;
- request time;
- request URL;
- network and request metadata;
- diagnostic information.

This information may be temporarily retained by infrastructure providers for security, reliability, debugging, abuse prevention, or service operation purposes.

Imbrel does not use this technical information for advertising or behavioral profiling.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by Imbrel is used only for purposes necessary to provide and maintain the App, including:

- retrieving weather forecasts;
- providing weather for the user's current location;
- searching for weather by city or location;
- caching weather information for offline or more efficient access;
- maintaining the security and reliability of network services;
- diagnosing technical errors and service failures.

## 3. Third-Party Services

Imbrel uses third-party services to provide its functionality.

### Cloudflare

Imbrel uses Cloudflare infrastructure, including Cloudflare Workers, as an intermediary between the App and the weather data service.

Requests sent through Cloudflare may include geographic coordinates or other weather-query parameters required to retrieve weather information.

Cloudflare may process network and technical information according to its own privacy practices.

Cloudflare Privacy Policy:
https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

### Meteosource

Imbrel uses Meteosource to obtain weather forecasts and related weather information.

Depending on how weather is requested, Meteosource may receive geographic coordinates or location identifiers required to return weather data.

Meteosource processes information according to its own terms and privacy practices.

Meteosource website:
https://www.meteosource.com/

### Android and Google Services

Imbrel relies on Android platform functionality and may use Google-provided Android services for functionality such as device location.

The processing of information by Google services is governed by Google's applicable privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Data Sharing

Imbrel does not sell personal information.

Information required to provide weather functionality may be transmitted to service providers described above.

These services process information only as necessary for providing their respective infrastructure, location, geocoding, or weather-related functionality, subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

## 5. Advertising

Imbrel currently does not contain advertising and does not use user location for advertising purposes.

If advertising functionality is added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when such functionality becomes available.

## 6. Analytics and User Accounts

Imbrel does not require users to create an account or sign in.

Imbrel currently does not create personal user profiles and does not use personal information for behavioral analytics or targeted advertising.

## 7. Data Retention

Weather forecasts, saved location information, and application preferences may be stored locally on your device.

Local information remains on the device until it is replaced, cleared through Android application settings, or removed when the App is uninstalled.

Imbrel does not maintain a developer-controlled database of user accounts.

Third-party infrastructure providers may temporarily retain network, security, and diagnostic information according to their own service configurations, retention periods, and privacy policies.

## 8. Data Security

Imbrel uses encrypted HTTPS connections when communicating with online services.

Reasonable technical measures are used to limit the transmission of information to what is necessary to provide App functionality.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Your Choices

You may:

- deny or revoke location permission at any time through Android settings;
- use manual location search instead of device location;
- clear locally stored App data through Android settings;
- uninstall Imbrel to remove locally stored application data.

Some features, particularly weather based on your current location, may not function when location permission is disabled.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Imbrel is a general-purpose weather application and is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

The App does not knowingly request users to provide their name, email address, or other account information.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Imbrel's functionality, third-party services, or data practices change.

Changes will be published on this page together with an updated effective date.
